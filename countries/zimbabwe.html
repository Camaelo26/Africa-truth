About Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe, formerly Rhodesia, gained independence in 1980 after a prolonged liberation struggle. Robert Mugabe led the country for 37 years until a military coup in 2017 ended his rule. The country endured hyperinflation and economic collapse from controversial land reform policies in the 2000s.

For more information, visit the Wikipedia page.
