About Eswatini (Swaziland)

Eswatini, formerly known as Swaziland until 2018, is Africa's last absolute monarchy. King Mswati III has ruled since 1986, banning political parties and concentrating all power in the throne. In 2021, pro-democracy protests were met with deadly force. Despite growing calls for reform, the monarchy maintains firm control over the small landlocked kingdom.
