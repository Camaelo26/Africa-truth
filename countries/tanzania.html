About Tanzania

Tanzania was formed in 1964 from the union of Tanganyika and Zanzibar. Its first president Julius Nyerere built the country on his Ujamaa philosophy — African socialism based on community and self-reliance. Though the policy had mixed economic results, Nyerere is widely respected as a pan-African visionary and one of Africa's most principled leaders.
