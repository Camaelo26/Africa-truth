About Djibouti

Djibouti gained independence from France in 1977. One of Africa's smallest countries, it sits at one of the world's most strategically important maritime chokepoints — the Bab-el-Mandeb strait. It hosts military bases from the US, France, China, Japan, and Italy. President Ismail Omar Guelleh has ruled since 1999, maintaining stability but limiting political freedoms.
