About Eritrea

Eritrea gained independence from Ethiopia in 1993 after a 30-year war of liberation. Since then it has been ruled by President Isaias Afwerki, who has established one of the world's most closed and repressive regimes — sometimes called "Africa's North Korea." The country has no elections, no free press, and enforces indefinite national service.
